Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Information we collect

LexiFlow AI may collect account details, contact information, uploaded documents, translation settings, payment status, and usage records needed to provide document translation services.

How we use information

  • To process uploaded documents and generate translations.
  • To maintain account access, billing records, and credit balances.
  • To detect abuse, troubleshoot issues, and improve service quality.
  • To respond to support, refund, and compliance requests.

Document content

Uploaded documents are used for translation and preview/export workflows. Users should not upload documents they are not authorized to process. LexiFlow AI does not sell uploaded document content.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay. LexiFlow AI stores payment order status, credit purchases, and transaction references. Card and bank details are handled by Razorpay and are not stored by LexiFlow AI.

Data retention

Account, billing, and translation records may be retained as needed for service continuity, support, legal compliance, and abuse prevention. Users may contact support to request deletion of eligible data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@lexiflow.ai.
