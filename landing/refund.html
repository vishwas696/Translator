Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Credit purchases

LexiFlow AI sells prepaid translation credits. Credits are added to a user's wallet after successful payment verification through Razorpay.

Refund eligibility

Refund requests may be considered for unused credit purchases made in error, duplicate payments, or payment failures where credits were not added. Requests should be sent within 7 days of payment.

Used credits

Credits consumed for completed translation jobs are generally not refundable because translation provider and processing costs are incurred when the job runs.

Cancellation

Users may stop using the service at any time. Since credits are prepaid, there is no recurring subscription to cancel unless a separate plan is introduced and disclosed.

Refund processing

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where possible. Payment processor timelines may vary.

Contact

For refund requests, contact support@lexiflow.ai with your account email, payment reference, and reason for the request.
